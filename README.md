# ⚙️ unity-gamedev-evolution

Welcome to my Unity game development portfolio. Here, I present two projects: one created by me in 2023, and the other developed in 2026 as part of a two-person team. We created all the models, sounds, music, and other assets ourselves.

---

### 👽 "Strange Spheres" (2023)
* **Development Type:** Solo Project (Developed at age 13)
* **Engine & Tools:** Unity, C#, Blender, Adobe Mixamo
* **Genre:** Time-Attack Arcade / 3D Collect-a-thon

**Description:**  
A dynamic 3D time-attack arcade game built independently at the age of 13 as a self-learning project to master Unity. The core gameplay challenge requires the player to explore a low-poly map and locate and collect exactly 100 scattered spheres before the real-time countdown timer runs out.

**Key Technical Features:**
* **Gameplay Logic & Scripting:** Programmed core C# mechanics including player movement, trigger-based collection system, and a dynamic real-time countdown timer using YouTube programming tutorials.
* **Game-Loop UI Assembly:** Constructed a complete game-loop interface directly inside Unity (including Start, Victory, and Game Over screens) linked to an active UI counter for tracked spheres and the remaining time.
* **3D Modeling:** Independently designed and modeled all environment objects and spheres from scratch in Blender using a clean Low-Poly art style.
* **Animations:** Integrated character assets and configured locomotion using the Adobe Mixamo platform and Unity Animator.
* **Audio Integration:** Sourced and configured background music and sound effects from open internet libraries to fit the high-stakes atmosphere.

### 👻 "Close Your Eyes" (2026)
* **Development Team:** SynthGlass (Indie Collective Co-Founded by Me & My Teammate)
* **Project Scale:** Developed Hands-On by a 2-Person Equal Partnership
* **My Role:** Team Co-Founder, Level Designer & Environment Artist
* **Engine & Tools:** Unity, C#, Blender, Figma, FL Studio
* **Genre:** Stealth-Horror / Survival

**Description:**  
An atmospheric 3D stealth-horror game developed under our indie brand **SynthGlass**. This specific project was driven and executed entirely by a 2-person equal partnership. Our roles were divided based on specialization: myself handling the full visual presentation and level design, and my co-founder managing the C# programming and audio production. The gameplay focuses on navigating a dark warehouse while managing a flashlight battery and avoiding a monster using a unique "close your eyes" mechanic.

**Key Technical & Design Features (My Contributions):**
* **Full Scene Production & Assembly:** Built, populated, and completed all corridors and warehouse environments within Unity, taking charge of the game's entire physical layout.
* **Level Design & Prefab Structure:** Fully designed the warehouse layout, structured optimized prefabs, and balanced the placement of obstacles and side rooms.
* **PSX-Style 3D Art Pipeline:** Independently created, modeled, and mapped 100% of the game’s 3D assets in Blender, strictly following a retro PS1-horror aesthetic with low-res pixelated textures.
* **Graphic Design in Figma:** Used Figma to design the game’s promotional artwork, cover banner, and the official **SynthGlass** team branding assets.
* **Visual Styling & Inspector Tweaking:** Adjusted retro pixel-art horror shaders directly within the Unity Inspector, tweaking pixelation parameters to perfectly match the PSX-style models and textures.
* **Code & Asset Integration:** Integrated the C# scripts and sound assets developed by my co-founder directly into the Unity project structure, fine-tuning inspector variables for game balance. Selected source scripts are included in this repository for architecture review.

**⚠️ Project Status & Version Control Post-Mortem:**
Before attempting a major Unity version migration, we successfully compiled the final working build of the game, which is why all the attached screenshots are captured directly from the live gameplay. However, during the subsequent update process, the engine suffered a severe asset conflict, corrupting the source files and breaking the project hierarchy inside the editor. While the project is technically restorable, the required time and effort to rebuild everything from scratch made it ineffective to pursue. Therefore, the project was officially frozen and archived.

This critical experience became my most valuable lesson in software development. It taught me the vital importance of data safety and prompted a complete shift in my workflow: from that exact moment onward, I integrated **Git and GitHub** into every single new project and task to ensure secure repository management, branching, and automated version control.
